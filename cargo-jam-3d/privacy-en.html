Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

In short: We do not collect or sell your personal data, and your game progress is stored only on your own device. The game is free, shows ads provided by Google AdMob and uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in aggregate, how the game is played. Google may collect certain device data for these purposes, as described below. Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. The game has no accounts.

1. Who we are

Cargo Jam 3D: Dock Rush ("the game") is developed by Deniz Kılınç and published on Google Play under the developer name "Aventra App" and on the App Store under the name Deniz Kılınç. For any privacy question, contact us at support@useaventra.com.

2. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following information only in your device's local storage. It is never sent to us.

DataPurpose
Level progress, coins, boosters, lives, daily reward, daily quest, win streak, piggy bank, star and achievement statusSaving your game progress
Settings (sound, music, vibration, notifications, language)Remembering your preferences
Purchase confirmations (product and transaction ID) and the "No Ads" statusDelivering what you bought exactly once and remembering permanent purchases

You can delete all of this data at any time by uninstalling the game or by clearing its data in your device settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free to play and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. Ads appear in two ways: rewarded ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. The game itself does not see or store any of the data listed below; it is collected and processed directly by Google.

When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA on iOS only if you allow tracking);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped;
  • diagnostic data related to ad delivery and performance.

Google uses this data to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse and, where you have consented, to show ads based on your interests. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

4. Game analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To improve difficulty, balance and stability, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. It records anonymous gameplay events, for example: a level was started, completed or failed (with the level number and type), a booster or "Continue" was used, coins were earned or spent, an ad was watched, a daily quest was completed or an in-app purchase was made. Together with these events Google may collect an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase, device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version), approximate location (country or region derived from the IP address) and usage information such as session length.

We do not send your name, email or any other information that directly identifies you, and we see this data only as statistics. Google keeps event data according to the retention settings of our Analytics account (at most 14 months). See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services for details. You can stop all analytics by uninstalling the game; on Android you can also reset your advertising ID, and on iOS analytics data is not used for tracking.

5. In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases with real money (for example coin packs, a starter pack, "No Ads" and the piggy bank). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies; we never receive your card or payment details. We receive only a confirmation of the purchase (product and transaction identifier), which the game stores on your device so the item is delivered once. Refunds are handled by the store you purchased from.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your own device (for example "Your lives are refilled" or "Your daily reward is ready"). No server is involved and no data is sent for this purpose. You can turn notifications off at any time in the game under Settings → Notifications or in your device settings.

7. Your choices about ads

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: before any ads are requested, the game shows Google's consent message so you can choose whether your data may be used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks for your permission before your device's advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you decline, the identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised using it. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings → Google (or Privacy) → Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. You can play the whole game without watching them. After buying "No Ads", ads between levels are no longer shown.

8. Data we receive

We, the developer, do not collect personal data that identifies you through the game. We receive only aggregated reports: from AdMob (for example, the number of ads shown and the resulting earnings), from Firebase Analytics (for example, how many players completed a level) and from the app stores (for example, sales totals).

9. Contacting support

If you email us (for example using the "Support" button in Settings), we receive your email address, the content of your message and anything you choose to include, such as your player ID. We use this information only to answer you and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

10. Sharing and external links

The "Share" button opens your device's own share menu; whatever you share is sent by the app you choose, under that app's own privacy policy. Links to this policy, the terms of use or email open in your browser or email app. Tapping an ad may open a website or store page operated by the advertiser, which has its own privacy policy.

11. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us by email, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) or the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent. Game progress is stored only on your device and is fully under your control. For data processed by Google for advertising and analytics, you can use the choices in sections 4 and 7 or contact Google. For any request about support emails you sent us, contact support@useaventra.com.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
