Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

In short: We do not collect or sell your personal data, and your game progress is stored only on your own device. The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure these ads, Google may collect certain device data, such as an advertising identifier, as described below. The game has no accounts and no in-app purchases.

1. Who we are

Cargo Jam 3D: Dock Rush ("the game") is developed by Deniz Kılınç and published on Google Play under the developer name "Aventra App" and on the App Store under the name Deniz Kılınç. For any privacy question, contact us at support@useaventra.com.

2. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following information only in your device's local storage. It is never sent to us.

DataPurpose
Level progress, coins, boosters, lives, daily reward and achievement statusSaving your game progress
Settings (sound, music, vibration, language)Remembering your preferences
Profile nickname and avatar you chooseShowing your profile inside the game
A random player ID created on your deviceShown in Settings so you can include it when you contact support; it is not linked to your identity

You can delete all of this data at any time by uninstalling the game or by clearing its data in your device settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free to play and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. Ads appear in two ways: rewarded ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. The game itself does not see or store any of the data listed below; it is collected and processed directly by Google.

When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA on iOS only if you allow tracking);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped;
  • diagnostic data related to ad delivery and performance.

Google uses this data to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse and, where you have consented, to show ads based on your interests. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

4. Your choices about ads

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: before any ads are requested, the game shows Google's consent message so you can choose whether your data may be used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks for your permission before your device's advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you decline, the identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised using it. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings → Google (or Privacy) → Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. You can play the whole game without watching them.

5. Data we collect

We, the developer, do not collect any personal data through the game and do not receive the advertising data described above in a form that identifies you. AdMob gives us only aggregated reports (for example, the total number of ads shown and the resulting earnings).

6. Contacting support

If you email us (for example using the "Support" button in Settings), we receive your email address, the content of your message and anything you choose to include, such as your player ID. We use this information only to answer you and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

7. Sharing and external links

The "Share" button opens your device's own share menu; whatever you share is sent by the app you choose, under that app's own privacy policy. Links to this policy, the terms of use or email open in your browser or email app. Tapping an ad may open a website or store page operated by the advertiser, which has its own privacy policy.

8. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) or the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent. Game progress is stored only on your device and is fully under your control. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can use the choices in section 4 or contact Google. For any request about support emails you sent us, contact support@useaventra.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes, for example if in-app purchases are added. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
